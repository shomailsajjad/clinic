ONLINE TESTING ON RENDER

This prepares a browser-based demo, not the finished clinical system.
The demo has no real patient data. Never enter real patient data.

1. Sign in at https://dashboard.render.com using GitHub.
2. Choose New > Blueprint and connect shomailsajjad/clinic.
   If the repository is private, grant Render access to that repository.
3. Render reads render.yaml and proposes a free testing web service.
   Confirm that the displayed plan is free before creating anything.
4. Set CLINIC_DEMO_ADMIN_PASSWORD in Render's secure form. Choose a unique
   password with at least 12 characters. Do not send the password in chat.
5. Create/deploy the blueprint and wait until the service is live.
6. Open the service's HTTPS address shown in Render. Your login username
   is admin; the password is the one you entered in Render.
7. Create Operator and Doctor accounts under Users & access.

The real web address is assigned by Render. No hosted demo is live until
Render confirms deployment. Free services may sleep and can be slow on
the first visit. Free service terms and availability are controlled by Render.

This testing demo uses temporary SQLite storage. Patients, prices and user
accounts can reset on restart/redeployment. A new admin is initialized from
your secure setting after a reset; existing admin passwords are not silently
overwritten. Persistent hosting and PostgreSQL are separate deployment work.

The demo includes login/roles, patients, services/pricing, users and an
activity log. Booking, generated tokens, finance, diagnostic reports and
backups remain unimplemented. The login throttle is per username, stored
in process memory, and resets when the service restarts. This is not a
production security or deployment configuration.

If deployment fails, share the error text from Render's Logs; omit secrets.
